# CSE Fest 2026 Videography Competition Rulebook
<!-- p.3 -->
## 1. Introduction and Eligibility Criteria
The IIUC CSE Festival 2026 Videography Competition is organized by the IIUC Computer Club in association with the Department of CSE, IIUC. It celebrates creativity, cinematic storytelling and technical excellence; participants create original, impactful short videos.
- Open for all IIUC students regardless of department.
- Participants may enter individually as a solo submission.
- Each participant may submit only one video entry.
- A participant cannot be part of multiple submissions.
<!-- p.4 -->
## 2. Theme & Content Guidelines
The theme is strictly open. Any positive, creative, educational, emotional, social, or cinematic concept is allowed. All content must respect university ethics and Islamic values.
Allowed content:
- Positive, creative, and uplifting narratives of any kind.
- Educational, social, documentary, or short-film style videos.
- Natural ambient sounds, voice-overs, dialogue, and foley sound design.
- Soft cinematic atmosphere and instrument-free nasheeds as background.
- Watermarks or logos, provided they do not obstruct the visuals.
Not allowed:
- Biographies of specific named persons.
- Depictions of any historical movement or political event.
- Excessive meme-style editing or TikTok / reel-type low-effort content.
- Any content that violates university ethics or Islamic values.
<!-- p.5 -->
## 3. Video Requirements
- Maximum duration: the video must not exceed 3 minutes in length.
- All footage must be original; no downloaded clips from YouTube or any external source.
- AI-generated video content is strictly prohibited in any form.
- Ensure good lighting, stable framing, and professionally clear visuals.
- Recommended resolution: 1080p Full HD or higher.

## 4. Audio Restrictions
Allowed audio: natural ambient sound; voice-overs and dialogue; foley sound design; soft cinematic atmosphere; instrument-free nasheeds.
Strictly prohibited: explicit songs or vulgar lyrics; EDM, DJ tracks, heavy beat music; copyrighted music (unlicensed); distracting or inappropriate SFX; music with musical instruments.
<!-- p.6 -->
## 5. Direct Disqualifications
Submissions containing any of the following will be immediately and unconditionally disqualified. The organizing committee's decision is final.
- Downloaded footage from YouTube or any external source.
- AI-generated video content of any form.
- Copyright violations (music, footage, or any other media element).
- Political propaganda or content showing religious disrespect.
- Vulgar, obscene, or sexually inappropriate scenes.
- Harassment, bullying, hate speech, or offensive language of any kind.
- Glorification of violence or drug use.
- Submission received after the official deadline.
- Incorrect file naming format (see Submission Rules).
- Failure to upload to both Google Drive and Facebook.
The organizing committee reserves the right to disqualify any entry deemed inappropriate, even if not explicitly listed.
<!-- p.7 -->
## 6. Submission & Posting Rules
Google Drive submission:
- Upload your final video to the official Google Drive link provided by organizers.
- Set file sharing to "Anyone with the link can view" before submitting.
- File naming format: Name_ID_Device_EditingSoftware (example: Sakif_C241268_CanonM50_CapCut).
Facebook upload (mandatory):
- Upload the video to your personal Facebook profile or page, with a short caption relevant to the video theme.
- Mention the device used for shooting (e.g. Shot on: iPhone 13) and the editing software (e.g. Edited with: CapCut).
- Add hashtags #iiuc_cse_fest_2026 and #IIUC_CC_VideographyContest, and tag the official IIUC Computer Club Facebook page.
Both the Google Drive upload and the Facebook post are mandatory. Missing either one will result in automatic disqualification.
<!-- p.8 -->
## 7. Rules & Regulations
General rules:
- All submitted videos must be 100% original and created solely by the participant.
- Plagiarism or use of others' work in any form is strictly prohibited.
- Participants must own or have legal rights to all media used in their video.
- Offensive, unethical, or inappropriate content is strictly not allowed.
- Participants must maintain professional and respectful conduct throughout.
- Late submissions will not be accepted under any circumstances.
- The organizing committee's decisions are final and binding in all matters.
- Any violation of these rules may result in immediate disqualification.
Participation rules:
- Each participant may submit only one video entry for the contest.
- Participants must be currently enrolled students of IIUC.
- Registration fee of 30 BDT must be paid before the deadline.
- Submitted videos must remain publicly visible on Facebook until the event ends.
- Participants are solely responsible for their own equipment and software.
