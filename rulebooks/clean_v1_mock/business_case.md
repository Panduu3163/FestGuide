## 01. Overview & Eligibility
Business Case Challenge (MOCK) is an invited business-strategy track created for non-engineering students during IIUC Fest 2026. This rulebook is mock demo data.
- Open exclusively to BBA students (Department of Business Administration).
- Team size: 3 to 5 members per team.
