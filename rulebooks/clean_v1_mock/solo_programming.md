# Intra-IIUC Solo Programming Contest | CSE Fest 2026
<!-- p.1 -->
Contest Type: Intra-University Programming Contest. Contest Mode: Onsite Solo Contest.

## 1. Eligibility & Participant Requirements
- The contest is open to all currently enrolled students of IIUC.
- Each participant must compete individually.
- Participants must be present before the contest starts. Late arrival after the reporting deadline may result in disqualification.

## 2. Contest Structure
Date & Time: June 14, 2026 at 10:00 AM. Duration: 3 to 3.5 hours. Format: ICPC-style scoring. Programming languages: C/C++, Java, Python. IDE: CodeBlocks, VS Code, Sublime Text. Problem order: unsorted.
<!-- p.2 -->
Note: Participants intending to use Java, Python, or VS Code or Sublime Text must ensure the required setup is done by themselves beforehand.

## 3. Ranking and Penalty System
The contest will follow the ICPC Regional Contest style scoring system for individual participants.
- Participants will be ranked primarily by the number of solved problems.
- In case of a tie, the participant with the lower total penalty time will be ranked higher.
- Penalty time for each solved problem is the time consumed until the problem is solved, plus 10 minutes for each wrong submission made before the accepted submission.
- Wrong submissions for unsolved problems will not incur any penalty.
- The official contest scoreboard at the end of the contest will determine the final rankings.

## 4. Reference Document/Template Rules
Participants are allowed to bring only one printed reference document/code template during the contest.
- The document may contain up to 20 pages of reference materials.
- Pages must be single-sided and either A4-sized or letter-sized.
- Each page must be numbered in the upper right corner.
- The participant's name should be written in the upper left corner.
- Text and illustrations must be clearly readable without magnification.

## 5. Conduct & Integrity Rules
For contest integrity, fairness and a smooth contest environment, some important decisions have been updated.
- Toph Only Environment: during the contest, participants are strictly prohibited from accessing any website other than Toph.
- Pre-Contest Restriction: websites other than Toph will be blocked a few hours before the contest starts.
- Participants should not keep any dependency on online resources. Prepare everything before coming to the venue.
- Not Allowed: AI tools, search engines, documentation websites, or any external online resources during the contest.
- Allowed: prepared hard copies / printed materials.
- Communication with anyone outside the contest officials is strictly prohibited during the contest.
- Participants must remain seated at their assigned places unless permitted otherwise.
- Participants can use the in-built calculator in the PC.
- Any form of hacking, system tampering, unauthorized access attempt, or use of any website/platform other than Toph during the contest will result in immediate disqualification.
- Damaging equipment, disruptive behavior, or violating contest integrity may lead to penalties or disqualification.

## 6. Allowed & Prohibited Items
Allowed items: university ID card; 1 printed reference document/code template; stationery (pen/pencil); sealed water bottle; necessary medications (must be declared during check-in).
Prohibited items: mobile phones; smart watches; tablets or laptops; personal keyboard or mouse; storage devices (USB drives, hard disks, etc.); additional books or printed materials (except the permitted reference document).
<!-- p.4 -->
## 7. Clarification Rules
- Participants are not allowed to communicate with other contestants during the contest.
- Any problem clarification request must be made through the official clarification system or contest officials. Organizers may publish clarification announcements if necessary.

## 8. Technical Issues Policy
- Any technical issue must be reported immediately to the volunteers or organizers.
- The contest clock will not stop for individual technical problems unless there is a major system-wide failure.

## 9. Final Authority
- The organizing committee reserves the right to modify, add, or remove any rule at any stage of the contest.
- All decisions made by the organizing committee and judging panel will be final and binding.
- By participating in the contest, contestants agree to follow all rules and decisions made by the organizers.
