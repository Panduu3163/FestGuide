# IIUC CSE Festival 2026 - Idea Generation Competition Rulebook
<!-- p.3 -->
## 01-02. Introduction and Eligibility Criteria
Welcome to the IIUC CSE Festival 2026 - Idea Generation Competition, organized by the IEEE Computer Society IIUC Student Branch Chapter in association with IIUC Computer Club and Department of Computer Science & Engineering. The competition encourages creativity, innovation, problem-solving and entrepreneurial thinking among students, who propose innovative and practical ideas that solve real-world problems.
- Open for all FSE students (Faculty of Science and Engineering).
- Participants may join individually or as a team.
- Team size: 3-4 members.
- A participant cannot join multiple teams.
<!-- p.4 -->
## 03. Competition Objectives
The competition nurtures a culture of innovation and entrepreneurial thinking. Participants are expected to demonstrate these competencies: identify real-life problems; generate innovative solutions; demonstrate feasibility; exhibit analytical thinking; convey impact and scalability.
<!-- p.5 -->
## 04. Idea Categories
Artificial Intelligence (AI); Cybersecurity; Software & Web Solutions; Smart Campus Systems; Healthcare Technology; Agriculture Technology; FinTech & Digital Banking; Islamic Technology Solutions; Robotics & IoT; Environmental Sustainability; Smart Transportation; E-commerce; Mobile Applications; Education Technology (EdTech); Social Impact Technology.
<!-- p.6 -->
## 05. Submission Requirements
A. Idea Title: a short and clear title of the project/idea.
B. Problem Statement: what problem exists, why it is important, who is affected.
C. Proposed Solution: how the idea solves the problem and what technology or approach will be used.
<!-- p.7 -->
D. Key Features: the main functionalities or unique aspects.
E. Expected Impact: social impact, business potential, educational or environmental benefits.
F. Future Scope: how the idea can grow or improve in the future.
<!-- p.8 -->
## 06. Submission Format
PDF document, 2-5 pages maximum. PowerPoint presentation (on that day). File naming format: TeamName_IdeaTitle.pdf (example: TechVision_SmartCampus.pdf).
<!-- p.9 -->
## 07. Presentation Guidelines (Final Round)
Shortlisted teams present their ideas before the judging panel: 7 minutes presentation, 3-minute Q&A session. Recommended structure: Introduction; Problem Identification; Proposed Solution; Technical Overview; Innovation & Uniqueness; Expected Impact; Future Scope; Conclusion.
<!-- p.10 -->
## 08. Rules & Regulations
- All submissions must be original.
- Plagiarism is strictly prohibited.
- Offensive or unethical ideas are not allowed.
- Teams must maintain professional behavior.
- Late submissions may not be accepted.
- Judges' decisions will be final.
- Teams must report on time during presentation rounds.
- Any violation of rules may lead to disqualification.
