# Tech Olympiad 2026 - Official Rulebook & Syllabus
<!-- p.1 -->
## Rules & Regulations
- Participants must arrive at the venue at least 30 minutes before the examination.
- A valid IIUC Student ID may be required for verification.
- Smartphones, smartwatches, tablets, earbuds, and other smart devices are strictly prohibited during the exam.
- Communication with other participants during the examination is not allowed.
- Any form of cheating, plagiarism, impersonation, or unfair means will result in immediate disqualification (may lead to public disclosure of the violation).
- Participants must follow all instructions given by organizers and invigilators.
- Disruptive behavior may result in removal from the competition.
- The decision of the organizing committee shall be final in all matters.

## Official Syllabus
- Everyday Math & Brain Teasers: core engineering mathematics (calculus, linear algebra, vectors); analytical reasoning, IQ puzzles and logical patterns.
- Tech Trends & Artificial Intelligence: basics of AI and generative AI tools; prompt engineering fundamentals; robotics, automation and technology trends.
- Everyday Circuits & Gadget Basics: Ohm's law; series and parallel circuits; logic gates (AND, OR, NOT).
- Logic & Flowcharts: flowchart interpretation; problem-solving using pseudocode.
- Basics of Internet & Connectivity: internet fundamentals; Wi-Fi and cellular networks; basic data communication concepts.
- Basic Science: Physics, Chemistry & ICT.

## Exam Format
MCQ + short conceptual questions. Questions are conceptual and do not require advanced coding syntax.
