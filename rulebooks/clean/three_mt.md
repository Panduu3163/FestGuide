# Three Minute Thesis (3MT) Competition - Rule Book & Guidelines, CSE Fest 2026
<!-- p.2 -->
## 1. Introduction and Eligibility
The Three Minute Thesis (3MT) Competition encourages CSE students to present their research ideas, innovative solutions and technical concepts in a clear, concise and engaging manner within only three minutes. Organized by the Department of CSE, IIUC.
- Open to all students of the Department of CSE, IIUC.
- Participation is strictly individual.
- Participants must complete the registration process before the deadline.
Eligible topics: Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Agentic AI, Cyber Security, Data Science, Software Engineering, IoT & Smart Systems, Robotics & Automation, Software Innovation.
<!-- p.3 -->
## 2. Competition Information
- Date: 14 June 2026 (Sunday).
- Time: female participants 10:30 AM; male participants 1:40 PM.
- Venue: CSE Main Building Conference Room.
- Registration fee: 100 BDT.
- Registration deadline: 12 June 2026 (Friday).
- Maximum 3 minutes: present your research within three minutes.
- Individual presentation; each participant presents individually.
- English only: presentations must be delivered in English.
- Professional conduct and no offensive content: content must be appropriate, ethical and respectful. All work must be original and properly cited.
- Arrive 30 minutes earlier: participants must arrive at least 30 minutes before the session.
<!-- p.4 -->
## 3. Presentation Structure
Follow a clear and logical structure: Introduction; Literature Review (summarize 3-5 key previous studies); Research Gap; Objectives; Methodology; Expected Outcome.
<!-- p.5 -->
## 4. Research Proposal Guidelines
- Literature review: analyze 3-5 related works; select recent and relevant publications.
- Research gap: identify gaps in existing studies; maximum of 4 well-defined gaps.
- Objectives: one main objective (goal) and a maximum of 3 specific objectives; clear, measurable and achievable.
- Methodology / workflow: describe the approach and steps, with a clear workflow.
- Expected outcome: expected results, contributions, and potential real-world impact.
<!-- p.6 -->
## 5. Judging Criteria and Academic Integrity
Evaluation is based on both the rubrics and the quality of the presentation. After all presentations a Q&A session may be held at the discretion of the judges; questions are relevant to the presented research only, and judges' decisions during Q&A are final.
Academic integrity: submit only your own original work; plagiarism in any form leads to immediate disqualification; give proper credit to all sources; AI tools may be used ethically and transparently, and misuse is strictly prohibited.
<!-- p.7 -->
## 6. Organizer Rights, Attendance and Disqualification
- The organizers reserve the right to modify any rules if necessary. The decision of the judges and organizers is final and binding. The organizers may cancel, postpone, or reschedule the event if required.
- Attendance: participants must arrive at least 30 minutes before the session. Registered participants must be present throughout the event. Late entry may lead to disqualification at the organizer's discretion. No participant may leave the venue during their presentation.
- All matters not covered in the rule book are at the discretion of the organizers.
- Disqualification conditions: exceeding the 3-minute time limit; presenting someone else's work without proper citation or permission; not following the competition rules; unethical behavior or disrespect towards others.
- Participation certificates will be provided to all participants.
