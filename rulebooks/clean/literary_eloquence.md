# IIUC Fest 2026 - English Literary & Eloquence Contest (MOCK Rulebook)
<!-- p.1 -->
## 01. Overview & Eligibility
English Literary & Eloquence Contest (MOCK) celebrates literary criticism, rhetoric, and dramatic reading. This rulebook is mock demo data.
- Open exclusively to ELL students (Department of English Language & Literature).
- Students from other departments are not eligible unless approved as an organizer exception.
- Solo participation only: team size 1 member.
- One entry per participant.
<!-- p.2 -->
## 02. Registration, Fee & Schedule
- Registration fee: 100 BDT per participant.
- Registration deadline: 10 June 2026.
- Competition date: 12 June 2026, from 14:30 to 16:30.
<!-- p.3 -->
## 03. Rounds & Rules
- Round 1: Prepared 4-minute literary monologue or critical commentary.
- Round 2: 2-minute impromptu eloquence response on a theme drawn on stage.
- External cue cards and AI-generated scripts during stage prep are not permitted.
