# Logical Legends Debate Championship 2026 - Official Rulebook
<!-- p.3 -->
## 01. Introduction
The Logical Legends Debate Championship 2026 is the premier debating tournament of IIUC CSE FEST 2026, organized by the IIUC Computer Club and the Department of CSE, IIUC. It fosters critical thinking, leadership and eloquent expression among students of International Islamic University Chittagong.
<!-- p.4 -->
## 02. Eligibility Criteria
- IIUC students only: open exclusively for currently enrolled students of International Islamic University Chittagong.
- Male and female: separate competitions for Male and Female categories with independent formats.
- Each participant must possess a valid IIUC Student ID and be actively enrolled.
- Participation requirements: valid IIUC Student ID card; active enrollment in the current semester; cross-department participation welcome; adherence to registration deadlines; verification by the organizing committee.

## 03. Team Formation
Each team has 3 members.
- Cross department allowed: students from different departments can form a team together.
- No multiple teams: one participant cannot join multiple teams. Each student may only register with a single team.
- No changes after registration: team composition must be finalized at registration. No substitutions or team changes are permitted after confirmation.

## 05. Tournament Structure
- Round 1, Preliminary: all registered teams compete. Top teams advance based on wins and speaker scores.
- Round 2, Quarter Final: top 8 teams compete. Winners advance to the Semi Finals.
- Round 3, Semi Final: top 4 teams face off. The best 2 advance to the Grand Final.
- Final, Grand Final: the championship showdown between the top two teams.
Advancement rules: teams advance based on win/loss record and cumulative speaker scores. Tie-break policy: total speaker points, then head-to-head results.
<!-- p.8 -->
## 06. Parliamentary Debate Format
British Parliamentary format. The motion is announced before each round; teams prepare arguments for or against the proposition. Preparation time is allocated before each debate. Each speaker has a designated speaking time limit, strictly enforced by the timekeeper. Points of Information (POIs) are allowed during designated periods; speakers may offer POIs to opponents, who may accept or decline. Detailed timing and procedures will be provided at the briefing session.
<!-- p.9 -->
## 07. Speaker Responsibilities
- Prime Minister: defines the motion, presents the government's case, establishes the framework and criteria for the debate.
- Leader of Opposition: rebuts the Prime Minister's case, presents the opposition's counter-arguments and alternative framework.
- Deputy Speakers: extend and develop their side's arguments with new analysis, examples and rebuttals.
- Government Whip: summarizes the debate, clarifies clash points, explains why government wins. No new arguments permitted.
- Opposition Whip: summarizes the debate from the opposition's perspective. No new arguments allowed.
Speaker order: Prime Minister, Leader of Opposition, Deputy Prime Minister, Deputy Leader of Opposition, Government Whip, Opposition Whip.
<!-- p.10 -->
## 08. Judging Criteria
Matter 50% (content, logic, evidence, analysis); Manner 30% (delivery style, persuasiveness, engagement); Method 20% (speech structure, time management, strategic positioning).
<!-- p.11 -->
## 09. Scoring System
- Individual speaker scores: each speaker is ranked 1st to 4th within their debate, points allocated accordingly.
- Team scores: combined individual scores determine the team total for each round.
- Win/loss: the team with the higher cumulative score wins the debate.
- Ranking: teams ranked by total wins first, then speaker points across all rounds.
- Tie breaking: total speaker points, then head-to-head results, then judge deliberation.
- Best Speaker award: the individual with the highest cumulative speaker score. The team that wins the Grand Final is crowned Logical Legends Debate Champion 2026.

## 10. Code of Conduct
Maintain professional behaviour; respect opponents and adjudicators; harassment of any kind is strictly prohibited; offensive or inappropriate language will result in penalties; compete fairly and honestly; good sportsmanship is expected. Violations may result in penalties or disqualification.

## 11. Rules & Regulations
1. Original arguments required: no pre-written speeches or prepared materials are permitted during debates.
2. No external assistance: participants may not receive external help during debates. All electronic devices must be turned off.
3. Attendance is mandatory for all registered team members. Absence may result in forfeit.
4. Reporting time: teams must report at least 30 minutes before their scheduled debate time. Late arrival may lead to disqualification.
5. Disqualification conditions: plagiarism, use of prohibited materials, disruptive behavior, or failure to report on time will result in immediate disqualification.
Organizer authority: the organizing committee reserves the right to make final decisions on all matters. Judges' decisions are final and binding.
