# IIUC Fest 2026 - Economics & Banking Policy Pitch (MOCK Rulebook)
<!-- p.1 -->
## 01. Overview & Eligibility
Economics & Banking Policy Pitch (MOCK) challenges students to propose actionable monetary, microfinance, or fiscal policies. This rulebook is mock demo data.
- Open exclusively to EB (Economics & Banking) and BBA students.
- Cross-department pairing between EB and BBA is permitted.
- Engineering departments (CSE, EEE, ETE, CE, CCE) are not eligible without an organizer exception.
- Team size: 1 to 2 members (solo or pair).
- A participant may join only one team.
<!-- p.2 -->
## 02. Registration, Fee & Schedule
- Registration fee: 150 BDT per participant.
- Registration deadline: 14 June 2026.
- Competition date: 16 June 2026, from 11:00 to 13:30.
<!-- p.3 -->
## 03. Format & Submission
- Submit a 2-page policy brief citing data sources at least 24 hours before the pitch.
- Final pitch: 6-minute policy briefing plus 3-minute panel Q&A.
