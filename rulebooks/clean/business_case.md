# IIUC Fest 2026 - Business Case Challenge (MOCK Rulebook)
<!-- p.1 -->
## 01. Overview & Eligibility
Business Case Challenge (MOCK) is an invited business-strategy track created for non-engineering students during IIUC Fest 2026. This rulebook is mock demo data.
- Open exclusively to BBA students (Department of Business Administration).
- Engineering and other departments (such as CSE, EEE, ETE, CE, CCE) are not eligible without an organizer exception.
- Team size: 2 to 4 members per team.
- A participant may join only one team in this event.
<!-- p.2 -->
## 02. Registration, Fee & Schedule
- Registration fee: 400 BDT per team (non-refundable).
- Registration deadline: 11 June 2026.
- Competition date: 13 June 2026, from 14:00 to 17:00 (afternoon slot).
<!-- p.3 -->
## 03. Format & Judging Criteria
- Round 1: 4-slide executive case deck analyzing a Bangladesh retail or FinTech growth challenge.
- Final Round: 8-minute boardroom pitch followed by 4-minute Q&A.
- Scoring: Problem framing (25%), Financial feasibility (35%), Strategy execution (25%), Delivery (15%).
